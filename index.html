WHY WE EXIST

Healthcare shouldn't feel confusing.

Knowing what a symptom is. Knowing what questions to ask. Knowing when it might be time to ask for help.

Health information can be confusing, especially when you're trying to understand something for the first time.

Sometimes you just want someone to explain something without making you feel like you should have already known it.

That's one of the reasons we created MedYOUcare.

We want people to have a place where they can learn, be curious, and ask questions without feeling embarrassed.

You deserve to feel informed about your health. ♡

WHO WE ARE

Meet MedYOUcare.

a little about us ♡

MedYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making healthcare education more accessible, understandable, and community-focused.

We're students ourselves, and we've seen how easy it can be to feel lost when it comes to healthcare information.

A symptom can lead to a dozen questions. A new medical term can send you searching through pages of information. Sometimes, you just don't know where to start.

We wanted to create something that could help make that first step a little easier.

MedYOUcare focuses on everyday health topics that don't always get enough attention. We want to share information that people can actually understand and connect with.

A lot of what we do right now happens online through Instagram and TikTok, where we share educational posts, research, and videos.

But we also have plans to bring MedYOUcare into our communities through events, educational sessions, activities, and hands-on learning.

WHY IT MATTERS

This is personal to us.

MedYOUcare comes from our own experiences and the communities we grew up around.

We've seen how important it is to have health information that feels accessible and understandable, especially for communities that may not always feel represented in healthcare conversations.

That's something we care deeply about.

We're not trying to replace healthcare professionals. We want to help people understand the basics, learn about different health topics, and feel more comfortable knowing when it's time to reach out for professional help.

We also believe that healthcare education should be something people can actually enjoy learning about.

We're learning too, and we're building MedYOUcare with a lot of care along the way. ♡

WHAT'S NEXT

We're just getting started.

there's a lot more we'd love to do ♡

Right now, we're spending a lot of time creating educational content and growing our community online.

But we don't want MedYOUcare to live only on a screen.

We hope to bring health education into schools, libraries, community centers, and local events.

We'd love to create interactive booths, health kits, activities, and educational sessions where people can learn, ask questions, and have fun along the way.

We're also hoping to connect with nonprofits, schools, youth organizations, and other community groups who care about making health education easier to access.

There's a lot we're still figuring out, and that's okay.

We're excited to keep learning, growing, and seeing where MedYOUcare takes us. ♡
