WHY WE EXIST

Healthcare shouldn't feel confusing.

Knowing what a symptom means. Knowing what questions to ask. Knowing when it might be time to reach out for help.

Sometimes, having the right information is the first step toward feeling more confident about your health.

That's why we created MedYOUcare.

WHO WE ARE

Meet MedYOUcare.

here's why we care ♡

MedYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making healthcare education more accessible, understandable, and community-focused.

We believe everyone deserves access to health knowledge and support — especially underserved and underrepresented communities that are often overlooked in everyday healthcare conversations.

Through educational outreach, creative media, research, and community engagement, we hope to help students and families feel more confident navigating their health, communicating their concerns, and knowing when to seek professional support.

♡

We want healthcare education to feel welcoming.

Not overwhelming. Not intimidating. Not something you need to already understand.

WHY MEDYOUCARE

Built with care. Built from experience.

MedYOUcare wasn't created from a distance.

As students who have seen firsthand how confusing or inaccessible healthcare information can feel for families and communities like ours, we wanted to create something that felt different.

We want to make health education easier to approach, while still respecting the importance of accurate, responsible information.

We're not here to replace healthcare professionals. We're here to help people feel more prepared to talk to them.

WHAT'S NEXT

We're just getting started.

and there's a lot more we want to do ♡

Right now, a big part of our work happens online — through educational posts, videos, and conversations on social media.

But we don't want MedYOUcare to live only on a screen.

As we grow, we're hoping to expand into more hands-on community education through interactive booths, health kits, informational sessions, activities for children, and partnerships with schools, libraries, nonprofits, churches, and youth organizations.

Our goal is to meet people where they are and make healthcare education feel more approachable in the spaces they already know and trust.
