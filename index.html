Who is medYOUcare?

medYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making healthcare education more accessible, understandable, and community-focused.

We believe everyone deserves access to health knowledge and support, especially underserved and underrepresented communities that are often overlooked in everyday healthcare conversations.

Through educational outreach, creative media, and community engagement, we help students and families feel more confident navigating their health, communicating symptoms, and seeking medical support when needed.

OUR GOAL

Breaking down barriers.

We want to create safe, informative, and accessible spaces where people can learn, ask questions, and feel supported.

By focusing on overlooked everyday health concerns, especially within underprivileged and minority communities, we hope to create a future where healthcare knowledge is easier to access for everyone.

WHY medYOUcare?

Because healthcare education is personal.

As students who understand firsthand how confusing or difficult healthcare information can feel for families from underserved communities, we believe education should feel approachable—not intimidating.

medYOUcare was created with the belief that people should not need to already understand the healthcare system in order to learn about their own health.

Our goal is not to replace healthcare professionals. Instead, we want to help people feel more informed, prepared, and confident when engaging with their own healthcare.
