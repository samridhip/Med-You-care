a little about us

Healthcare knowledge
should belong to everyone.

And we're trying to make it a little easier to find.

medYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit focused on making healthcare education more accessible, understandable, and community-focused.

We create educational content, share research-based information, and bring health conversations into spaces where people already learn, connect, and feel comfortable.

From Instagram posts and short videos to future community events and hands-on activities, we want health education to feel less intimidating and more approachable.

"You shouldn't need to be a healthcare expert to understand your own health."

why medYOUcare?

This is personal to us.

We started medYOUcare because we've seen firsthand how different access to healthcare information can be depending on the community you're from.

Growing up in communities that are often underrepresented, we noticed that important health information isn't always easy to find, easy to understand, or shared in ways that actually feel relatable.

And we know what that feels like.

That's why we're creating something we wish more people had: a welcoming place to learn, ask questions, and become more comfortable talking about health.

We're not here to replace doctors or healthcare professionals. We're here to help people learn the basics, recognize when something might matter, and feel more prepared to seek professional help when they need it.

what we believe

Learn it.

We want young people and families to feel more confident understanding health information, communicating symptoms, asking questions, and knowing when it may be time to reach out to a healthcare professional.

okay, but why us? ♡

Because we've been on the other side of the question.

We know that healthcare can sometimes feel overwhelming, especially when the information you're looking for isn't written with you in mind.

We're students too. We're learning, researching, asking questions, and figuring things out just like everyone else. That's a big part of why we care about making health education feel more approachable.

We're building medYOUcare from our own experiences and from what we've seen in our communities. We hope it can become something that makes someone feel a little less confused, a little more informed, and a little more comfortable asking for help.
