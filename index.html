A LITTLE SOMETHING

Health information should feel like something you can actually understand.

Not a giant wall of medical words. Not something that makes you scared to ask a question.

Just information that helps you feel a little more informed, a little more comfortable, and maybe a little more confident about your next step.

That's the kind of space we're trying to create. ♡

WHO WE ARE

A little about us.

okay, here's our story ♡

MedYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making healthcare education more accessible, understandable, and community-focused.

We're students ourselves, and this project is something that comes from the communities we've grown up in.

We wanted to create a space where someone could come across a health topic, be curious about it, and actually find information that makes sense.

Right now, we're doing a lot of that through social media. We create educational Instagram posts, TikToks, videos, graphics, and research-based content.

But we don't want to stop there.

We're hoping to eventually bring MedYOUcare into schools, libraries, community centers, and local events too.

♡

You don't have to know everything before you start learning.

and that's okay.
THIS IS PERSONAL TO US

Why we care. ♡

this part means a lot to us

MedYOUcare comes from our own experiences and the communities we grew up in.

We've seen how some communities don't always have the same access to health information or resources. Sometimes the information exists, but finding something simple and understandable can still be difficult.

We've experienced parts of that ourselves, and that's something we want to work toward.

We know we're not going to solve every problem in healthcare. That's not what we're trying to do.

We're not here to replace doctors, nurses, or other healthcare professionals.

We just want to help people learn and feel a little more comfortable starting those conversations.

♡ We care about this because we've been there too.

WHAT'S NEXT

We're just getting started.

there's a lot more we'd love to do ♡

Right now, MedYOUcare mostly lives online.

But we have a lot we'd love to do beyond a screen.

We want to bring educational sessions into schools and community spaces.

We want to create interactive activities that people can actually participate in, not just read about.

We want to make health kits people can take home, create booths at community events, and connect with organizations that care about the same things we do.

There's still a lot we're figuring out.

And honestly, we're excited about that.

This is only the beginning. ♡
