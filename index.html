WHY WE EXIST

Healthcare shouldn't feel confusing.

Knowing what a symptom is. Knowing what questions to ask. Knowing when it might be time to ask for help.

Sometimes, health information can feel like a lot. There are medical words you don't understand, pages of information to sort through, and questions you don't always know how to ask.

And sometimes, you just want someone to explain something without making you feel like you should have already known it.

That's one of the reasons we created MedYOUcare.

We want to make healthcare education easier to understand and easier to connect with, especially when it comes to the everyday health questions people don't always talk about.

You deserve to feel informed about your health. ♡

WHO WE ARE

A little about us.

hi, we're MedYOUcare ♡

MedYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to making healthcare education more accessible, understandable, and community-focused.

We're students ourselves, and a lot of what we care about comes from the communities we've grown up in.

We've seen how some communities don't always have the same access to health information or resources. Sometimes even finding a simple, understandable explanation can be harder than it should be.

We've experienced some of that ourselves, and it's one of the reasons we wanted to create MedYOUcare.

We focus on health topics that people might be curious about, worried about, or simply want to understand better.

Right now, we're sharing what we're learning through educational Instagram posts, TikToks, videos, graphics, and research-based content.

But we're hoping to take MedYOUcare beyond a screen too.

We're working toward community events, educational sessions, interactive booths, health kits, and hands-on activities that can make learning about health feel more personal and engaging.

THIS IS PERSONAL TO US

Why we care. ♡

MedYOUcare comes from our own experiences and the communities we grew up in.

We've seen how some communities don't always have the same access to health information, resources, or even simple explanations about things that affect them every day.

We've experienced some of that ourselves, and it's one of the reasons we wanted to create MedYOUcare.

We know we can't fix every problem in healthcare, and that's not what we're trying to do.

We're not here to replace doctors or other healthcare professionals.

We just want to help people learn.

To make a health topic a little easier to understand. To help someone recognize a symptom. To give someone an idea of what questions they might want to ask. And hopefully, to make reaching out for professional help feel a little less intimidating.

We care about this because we've been there too. ♡

WHAT'S NEXT
