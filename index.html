WHY WE EXIST

Healthcare shouldn't feel confusing.

Knowing what a symptom is. Knowing what questions to ask. Knowing when it might be time to ask for help.

Health can be complicated, and finding information that you actually understand isn't always easy.

Sometimes, you just want someone to explain something without making you feel like you should have already known it.

That's where we hope MedYOUcare can help.

You deserve to feel informed about your health. ♡

WHO WE ARE

Meet MedYOUcare.

here's why we care ♡

MedYOUcare is a student-led nonprofit created because we care about making healthcare education easier to understand.

We're students ourselves, so we understand that health information can sometimes feel overwhelming. A new symptom, a medical term, or even a simple health question can lead to so many more questions.

We wanted to create a space where people can learn about their health without feeling lost or embarrassed to ask questions.

We especially care about communities that may not always feel represented in everyday healthcare conversations. Our goal is to make health education more accessible, understandable, and welcoming for everyone.

A lot of our work currently happens online. We create educational posts and videos, research health topics, and share information through Instagram and TikTok.

But we don't want MedYOUcare to stay only on a screen.

♡

We want people to feel comfortable learning.

Health questions are normal. Being curious about your health is normal. And asking for help is okay.

We want MedYOUcare to be a place where people can start those conversations.

WHY MEDYOUCARE

This means a lot to us.

MedYOUcare comes from our own experiences and the communities we grew up around.

We've seen how difficult it can be to find health information that feels understandable, relevant, and easy to access. Sometimes, important information is there, but it isn't explained in a way that feels welcoming.

That's something we want to change.

We care about creating content that people can actually learn from while still taking healthcare information seriously.

We are not here to replace doctors, nurses, or other healthcare professionals.

We want to help people feel more comfortable learning about their health, asking questions, and knowing when to reach out for professional support.

WHAT'S NEXT

We're just getting started.

and we have a lot more planned ♡

Right now, a big part of MedYOUcare happens online through educational posts, videos, and conversations.

But we want to bring our work beyond social media.

As we grow, we hope to host interactive booths and educational sessions at schools, libraries, community centers, and other community spaces.

We also want to create health kits, hands-on activities, stickers, and educational materials that can make learning about health more engaging, especially for younger students.

We're also interested in working with nonprofits, churches, youth organizations, schools, and other community groups that share our interest in making healthcare education more accessible.

There's still a lot we want to learn, create, and improve. We're excited to keep growing MedYOUcare and see where it takes us.
